Why Water Matters

Water plays a critical role in nearly every function of the human body and the planet

🧠

Brain Function

Your brain is roughly 75% water. Staying hydrated improves concentration, memory, and mood. Even mild dehydration can impair cognitive performance.

❤️

Heart Health

Water helps regulate blood pressure and heart rate. Proper hydration keeps blood flowing efficiently and reduces strain on the cardiovascular system.

💪

Physical Performance

Muscles are about 80% water. Dehydration leads to reduced endurance, increased fatigue, and cramping during physical activity.

🌡️

Temperature Regulation

Sweating and respiration release water to cool the body. Without adequate water intake, your body can overheat dangerously.

🫁

Digestion & Detox

Water aids digestion, dissolves nutrients, and flushes toxins through the kidneys. It prevents constipation and supports liver function.

🌱

Ecosystems & Agriculture

Freshwater sustains all terrestrial ecosystems. Agriculture accounts for 70% of global freshwater use, making water essential for food security.

Save Water Every Day

Simple actions that make a big difference

  • 🚿
    Take shorter showers
    Reducing your shower by 2 minutes saves up to 40 litres of water.
  • 🚰
    Fix leaking taps
    A dripping tap can waste over 20,000 litres per year.
  • 🥤
    Use a reusable bottle
    Plastic bottle production uses 3 litres of water per litre of bottled water.
  • 🌧️
    Collect rainwater
    Use it for gardening and reduce your municipal water use.
  • 🍽️
    Run full loads
    Dishwashers and washing machines are most efficient when full.
  • 🌿
    Water plants wisely
    Water early morning or late evening to reduce evaporation.
